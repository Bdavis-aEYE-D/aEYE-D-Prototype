How to take a great photo

Better photos mean more accurate results.

⭐ Best quality method
🧴 The Mirror Technique
  • Stand in front of a mirror near a window or well-lit room.
  • Use Rear Camera — higher resolution than front.
  • Enable flash. Hold phone 10–15 cm from your eye.
  • Watch the mirror reflection to frame your eye on screen.
  • Tap volume button to shoot — steadier than tapping screen.
  • Tap the iris on screen to lock focus before shooting.
💡
Light from the side
A window to your side gives even light without harsh reflections.
🎯
Tap to lock focus
Tap your iris on screen before shooting to focus on it, not the lashes.
👀
Eyes wide open
More iris visible = more data = better results.
👓
Remove glasses
Frames and reflections make detection much harder.
