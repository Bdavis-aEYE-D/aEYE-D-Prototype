How to take a great photo

Better photos mean more accurate results.

💡
Use diffuse, even lighting
Avoid facing windows or bright lamps — they cause reflections on the eye. Overcast outdoor light or a well-lit room with light from multiple directions works best.
📏
Hold at arm’s length
About 18–24 inches away. Your full face should be in the frame — the app will zoom into each eye automatically.
🎯
Lock focus on your eye
Tap your eye on the phone screen before shooting to make sure the camera focuses there, not on the background.
🧐
Look straight at the camera
Face the lens squarely and open your eyes wide. Both eyes visible in the same photo is ideal.
⏸
Hold still
Even slight blur reduces accuracy. Brace your elbow or rest your hand against something steady.

What this prototype proves

A classical-CV iris analyzer running entirely in the browser. Full-face capture, tap to locate either eye, zoom and fit, and a Davis Card per eye — no server, no ML, no photo leaves your phone.
