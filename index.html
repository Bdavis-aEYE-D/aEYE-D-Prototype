How to take a great photo

Better photos mean more accurate results.

⭐ Best quality method
🧴 The Mirror Technique
  • Stand in front of a mirror near a window or in a well-lit room.
  • Use Rear Camera in eyeD — the rear lens is higher resolution than the front.
  • Enable the flash to light your iris. Hold the phone 10–15 cm from your eye.
  • Watch the mirror reflection to frame your eye in the centre of the screen.
  • Tap the volume button to shoot — steadier than tapping the screen.
  • Focus on the iris, not the lashes. Tap the iris on screen to lock focus first.
💡
Light from the side, not behind you
A window to your side gives even, natural light without harsh reflections. Avoid facing a bright window — it creates glare spots on the iris that block color.
🎯
Tap the iris to lock focus
Before shooting, tap your iris on the camera screen. This tells the camera to focus on the iris, not the lashes or background.
👀
Eyes wide open
Look straight at the lens and open your eyes as wide as comfortable. More iris visible = more data = better results.
⏸
Hold still — brace yourself
Rest your elbow on a surface or brace your hand against your face. Even a millimetre of blur softens the iris detail the app reads.
👓
Remove glasses
Frames and lens reflections make it much harder to detect the iris. Take the photo without them when possible.
🖼️
Already have a good photo? Use it
Clear portraits, headshots, or close-up eye photos all work. Tap Choose Photo to upload from your camera roll.
